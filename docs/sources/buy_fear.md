﻿Buy the Fear, Sell the Greed
Seven strategies — entry, scale-in and exit rules
1. RSI PowerZones
Market / direction: Long SPY; also studied on equity ETFs.
Entry: Price closes above its 200-day simple moving average and 4-period RSI closes below 30. Buy on the close.
Scale-in: Buy one additional equal unit if 4-period RSI closes below 25 while the position is open.
Exit: Sell the entire position on the close when 4-period RSI closes above 55.
Alternative: Enter below RSI 25 and add below RSI 20; retain the RSI-above-55 exit.
2. CRASH
Market / direction: Short stocks.
Setup: Closing price greater than $5; 21-day average daily volume at least 1 million shares; 100-day historical volatility at least 100%; closing ConnorsRSI at least 90.
Entry: On the next trading day, short using a sell-limit order 3% or 5% above the signal-day close. These are separate entry variants.
Exit: Cover on the close when ConnorsRSI closes below 30.
Alternatives: ConnorsRSI exit below 20; historical-volatility minimums of 60% or 80%.
3. Vol Panics
Market / direction: Short VXX.
Entry: VXX closes above its 5-day simple moving average and its 4-period RSI is above 70. Short on the close.
Position variants: Basic — short one unit. Double — add one equal unit if a later close is above the initial entry-day close. 2-3-5 — short 20%, then 30%, then 50% of the planned full position. 1-2-3-4 — short 10%, then 20%, then 30%, then 40%.
Scale-in condition: In either staged variant, each later addition requires a close above the most recent actual entry price. The percentages are incremental allocations of the planned full position.
Exit: Cover the entire held position on the close when VXX closes below its 5-day simple moving average.
Alternative exit: Cover when VXX’s 4-period RSI closes below 20.
4. VXX Trend Strategy
Market / direction: Short VXX.
Entry: Short when the 10-day simple moving average crosses below the 30-day simple moving average.
Exit: Cover when the 10-day simple moving average crosses above the 30-day simple moving average.
Alternative: Use 10-day and 30-day exponential moving averages for both signals. Keep the same moving-average type for entry and exit.
5. Trading New Highs
Market / direction: Long stocks.
Setup: Closing price greater than $5; 21-day average daily volume greater than 1 million shares; a 52-week intraday high occurred within the past 20 trading days; ConnorsRSI below 15.
Entry: On the next trading day, buy using a limit order 7% or 10% below the signal-day close. These are separate entry variants.
Exit: Sell on the close when ConnorsRSI closes above 70.
The 52-week-high condition uses the daily high, not the closing price.
6. TPS — Time, Price, Scale-in
Market: Non-leveraged, non-inverse ETFs; the book’s test universe uses a 250,000-share daily-volume minimum over the preceding 20 trading days.
Long
Entry: ETF above its 200-day simple moving average and 2-period RSI below 25 for two consecutive closes. Buy 10% of the planned full position on the second close.
Scale-in: On a later close below the most recent actual entry price, add 20%. On another later qualifying close, add 30%; on another, add 40%. Do not add when the ETF closes below its 200-day simple moving average.
Exit: Sell the entire held position on the close when 2-period RSI closes above 70.
Short
Entry: ETF below its 200-day simple moving average and 2-period RSI above 75 for two consecutive closes. Short 10% of the planned full position on the second close.
Scale-in: On a later close above the most recent actual entry price, short another 20%. On another later qualifying close, add 30%; on another, add 40%. Do not add when the ETF closes above its 200-day simple moving average.
Exit: Cover the entire held position on the close when 2-period RSI closes below 30.
For both directions: Incremental allocations are 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, producing cumulative exposure of 10%, 30%, 60%, 100%. Additions need not occur on consecutive days. Exit whatever portion is held when the exit signal occurs.
7. Terror Gaps
Market / direction: Long ETFs, including leveraged ETFs; 20-day average daily volume of 250,000 shares.
Setup: The ETF’s previous closing ConnorsRSI is below 5.
Entry: The next session opens below the previous close, then sells off further intraday. Buy at the selected additional pullback: 1%, 1.5%, 2%, or 2.5% below the gap-down level. These are separate entry variants.
Exit: Sell on the close when ConnorsRSI closes above 70.
Entry precision: The book describes the percentage pullback as occurring after the gap-down opening; it does not print a separate entry-price formula.
